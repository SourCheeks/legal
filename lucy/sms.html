Lucy: text line by Alvin Liu

Lucy is the personal assistant text line run by Alvin Liu, a sole proprietor. It answers texts sent to (213) 297-8800. The people who use it are Alvin himself and family members and colleagues he has given the number to. Messages are replies to what you ask, plus reminders and status updates you request.

How to opt in

Text START to (213) 297-8800 to receive messages from Lucy, Alvin Liu's assistant line. Texting the number with your own question also opts you in to receiving replies.

Message frequency varies with your conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See the Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.

You only receive messages after you text the number first. Lucy does not text people who have not opted in, and numbers are never bought or rented.
